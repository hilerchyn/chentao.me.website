Product talk jumps to a list of features. As the list grows, who is using it, and what they take when they leave, goes faint.

I write three sentences first: who arrives, with what problem, and what they take when they leave. If the three sentences will not fill, it is not time to draw the interface. This site's home page was written that way: a visitor should be able to open a repo, read one page of notes, and never sign up.

When the path on paper is short, you can see what to cut while building. Features can wait. The product has changed only when those three sentences change.

Sketch it clearly, then decide which piece to make.
