title = "The model is a pen. The problem is yours."
date = "2026-11-03"
summary = "Use the model as a tool. The problem, the edges, and the facts you must check still have to be written down by you."
+++

A model writes fast. It also writes unchecked sentences so they look true. Numbers, titles, case studies: once you let it fill those in, the page holds things that cannot stand.

I keep two rules. First, facts come only from a source you can open; if it is missing, it stays off the page. Second, write the problem before you ask the model, and the output has to map back to those sentences. The resume and the notes on this site follow that: if it cannot be opened on GitHub, it does not go on the page.

AI is useful for drafts, comparison, and another way of saying it. It cannot choose the edges for you, and it cannot do the checking.

You can change the pen. The problem stays in your hands.
